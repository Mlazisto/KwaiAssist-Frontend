# KwaiAssist Product Truth (PRODUCT.md)

## 1. Durable Product Mission
KwaiAssist is an enterprise-grade conversational intelligence platform tailored specifically for South African service professionals, emergency trades, medical practices, boutique hospitality, and specialized consultancies.

It replaces missed calls and manual quoting with immediate, 24/7 WhatsApp customer conversations — calculating regional rate cards, travel buffers, and booking confirmations in real time.

## 2. Target Audience & Geography
- **Audience**: South African business owners, operations managers, practice managers, trades dispatchers, and boutique hospitality hosts.
- **Geography**: Primary metros across South Africa — Johannesburg (Gauteng), Cape Town (Western Cape), Durban (KwaZulu-Natal), Pretoria, and surrounding regional hubs.
- **Operating Context**: High WhatsApp penetration (96%+ of smartphone users in SA). Customers expect immediate quotes and hate waiting for email callbacks or dealing with voicemail.

## 3. Brand Voice & Tone
- **Tone**: Quietly authoritative, sophisticated, warm, and hyper-competent.
- **Style**: Editorial typography paired with tactile operational tools. No generic "AI hype", no purple gradient glow, no cartoonish illustrations.
- **Language**: English with authentic South African conversational context (ZAR pricing `R`, VAT compliance, local suburbs, emergency call-outs, loadshedding/solar diagnostics).

## 4. Key Trust Anchors
- **SARS Compliant**: Generates complete itemized VAT tax invoices.
- **POPIA Compliant**: Lawful data processing, opt-in integrity, encrypted local storage.
- **Meta Cloud API**: Verified official WhatsApp Business Cloud API infrastructure.
- **Guaranteed Response**: Under 5-second quotation and booking coordination.
